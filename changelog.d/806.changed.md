Every process of a Muru instance names its instance: a host's, a log relay's and a worker's process title ends with it in parentheses, `muru host shop@r3 (user)` or `muru worker blog@r1 default/0 (run:8000)`, a daemon started with its own `--state-dir`, and its edge, add its short instance ID, and every process the daemon, a run or a serve starts, and everything those start, has `MURU_INSTANCE_ID` and `MURU_INSTANCE` in its environment.
