On Linux each worker raises its own `oom_score_adj` to 500 as it starts, so the kernel's out-of-memory killer picks a worker, which its host replaces, before a host, the edge, the daemon or another service; `status --processes --json` gives each process's `oom_score_adj`. The systemd unit sets `OOMPolicy=continue`, since systemd's default stops the whole service when the kernel kills one worker, and carries `MemoryMax=` and `CPUQuota=` as commented-out lines; the systemd guide's new limits section says what a limit on the unit does.
