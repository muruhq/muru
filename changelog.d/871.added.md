Added `RestartRelease` to the admin API, which restarts every worker of a release, or of one worker group, in one call: the release's host replaces each worker in its turn under the group's `max_surge` and `max_unavailable`, so the restart carries on while the daemon is stopped or upgrading. It returns once the workers are marked, or waits when asked, the events `restart.started`, `worker.restarted` and `restart.finished` follow it, `muructl release show` says how far it has got, and `CancelRestart` leaves the workers not yet replaced. It is apart from `Debug`, so a client can be allowed to restart workers and nothing else.
