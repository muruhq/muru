The `[build]` and `[command NAME]` lines the daemon writes into a release's log carry the full UTC time, as every other line of the file does, so `muructl debug bundle --since` keeps them and they sort with the lines around them. Under `muructl run` and `muructl serve`, a build's lines follow the same rules for times as Muru's other lines, and with `--log-format json` a build's lines and a command's are records of kind `build` and `command`, the latter with the command's name.
