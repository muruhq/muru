Added path-scoped detours: `muructl detour run --path PREFIX` (or `--mount NAME`) and `muructl detour traffic --path PREFIX` make a detour take only the requests under those client URL paths, for previews and every traffic choice alike, leaving the rest to the serving release, and `--all-paths` takes every path again. Two detours of one application may take all of it under paths that do not overlap.
