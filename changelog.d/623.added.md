`muructl serve` applies a project's scaling policy, `[tool.muru.scaling]` or a group's `scaling` table, as the daemon does, with a scale-out admitted only when one more worker fits under the container's `memory.max` less the headroom, a worker counting at its group's `hard_limit` when one is set. Short of memory, the memory guard scales a group with a policy in by its largest worker rather than replacing it, never below `min_workers`. `muructl scale status` works inside the container, with the application optional; `scale pin` and `unpin` say they are not offered there, and a `--min-ready` above a group's `min_workers` is refused at start.
