`muructl overlay promote` makes the release an overlay pins the application's serving release, as a deploy would, rather than writing a pin into the route that later deploys never moved and the next apply undid. It refuses an overlay that pins only some of the application's routes, a client class's route, or splits traffic, and it prints what to put in the applied files for the next apply to keep the promotion. `muructl release list` and `muructl scale status` show a release's share of each route that splits traffic on a `traffic:` line.
