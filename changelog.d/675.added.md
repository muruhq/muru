Added `access_log_query`, in the `[logs]` table and on a virtual host, which keeps (the default), masks or drops the query string of the access log's targets and redirects in either format: `mask` writes the values of secret parameters such as `token` as `***`, and `drop` writes the path alone. `muructl run` and `muructl serve` take it as `--access-log-query` or `access_log_query` in their tables.
