A worker is numbered within its group everywhere it is shown: `muructl status --processes` and its JSON, `/v1/processes` and the dashboard's worker pages, the answers of `muructl debug` and `muructl restart`, the host's log lines and events, the tags of the release log (`muru.worker[reports/1]`), the access log and the metrics, matching its process title and its socket's name. `--worker` and the dashboard's `worker=` take `N` for a worker of the default group and `GROUP/N` for one of a named group, and the debug and restart answers carry the worker's `group` beside its `index`.
