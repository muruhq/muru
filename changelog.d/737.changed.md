The toolbar's Timing panel drew moments as moments and repeated waits one by one: the response head reaching the edge and the response starting became events, shown as a diamond with a dashed line through every row, the application's own time became "running the application", every wait for the GIL and every stretch with the event loop held was drawn as a segment on its row, and a tooltip on every bar, segment and event opened on hover, keyboard focus and tap. `muru.panels.Span` took `at` for an event and `segments` of the new `muru.panels.Segment`, drawn the same way in panels of your own.
