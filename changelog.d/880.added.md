Added `muructl detour preview [DETOUR]` and `--open-preview` on `muructl detour run`: a one-time entry link, valid for a minute, gives a browser a preview cookie carrying a secret the daemon made for the detour, and leads to the application's address; `--host`, `--list`, `--print` and `--header` give another host's link, a link for each address, a link to print, or the `Muru-Preview` header for curl. `/_muru/preview/leave` leaves preview, and a cookie of a detour that has ended is let go.
