Added `muructl rollout canary --capacity shared`, for upgrading on a node with no room for two full copies of an application: the baseline and the candidate share the worker count the baseline has when the rollout starts, each sized by its share of the traffic and never below one worker. At each step the candidate is grown and ready before the traffic moves and the baseline drains after it, within the node's budget; where the budget has no room the baseline gives up `max_unavailable` workers at a time first. A rollback grows the baseline back at once, and the rollout's pins are lifted when it ends. Each move is a `rollout.capacity` event, and a managed HTTP server release is refused.
