Added `muructl debug bundle`, which writes one archive of a whole system for a time window (`--since`), for the people who own it to triage with: its logs and events, the configuration in force and what the workers are doing. It works with the daemon stopped, records what it could not collect in its manifest, writes to a file only its owner can read or to standard output (`--output -`), and with `--dry-run` says what it would hold.
