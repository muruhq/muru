Fixed the daemon stopping everything else while a release started: a release's build, preload, `before_fork` gates and wait for its workers, and a promotion's `before_promote` gates, now run beside the daemon's loop, so `muructl status`, other applications' deploys and the daemon's own checks go on meanwhile. A drain or removal of a release that is starting cancels its start, and a scale of it is refused until it is ready.
