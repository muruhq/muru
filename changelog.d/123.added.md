Added named conditions: `[condition.NAME]` with `when`, `for`, `clear_when`, `clear_for` and `min_requests`, in the daemon's file or an applied file, written in a small PromQL-shaped language over Muru's own figures (`error_rate`, `request_share`, `latency`, `queue_depth` and more). The daemon evaluates each every second and raises `condition.firing` and `condition.resolved` events, and `muructl condition list` and `muructl condition show` give their states and figures.
