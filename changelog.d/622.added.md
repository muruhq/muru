`muructl probe live`, `ready` and `startup` ask the daemon as well as `muructl serve`, over its admin socket, with `--app` and `--min-ready`, exiting 1 with the reason on standard error; the daemon answers the same as `/livez`, `/readyz` and `/startupz` on its metrics address. An application with no serving release does not hold readiness back. The daemon reads its own cgroup's limits and, with a `memory.max`, its memory guard replaces the largest worker of any release, or scales its group in when its policy allows, and `muructl status` shows the limits. The daemon guide's "In a container" section covers a container that is kept: volumes, logs, stopping, probes, the admin socket, memory limits and a Compose service.
