Under `muructl serve` in a container with a memory limit, a memory guard replaces the largest worker, one at a time, when less than the headroom (10% of `memory.max`, at least 128MB) is left, reported as `worker.recycled` with the reason `container_memory`, and holds off with a warning when replacing workers would not help. The limits are read at every memory check, so `docker update` or an in-place pod resize applies without a restart; the start lines give the limits read and warn when the workers are more than the CPUs, when a preload would not fit once each worker holds a copy of it, when hard limits add up to more than the limit, and when request bodies are spooled to memory, whose quota is then the headroom; and `muructl status`, `/status` and their JSON show the limits, what is used and left, and what the guard is doing.
