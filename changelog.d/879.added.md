Added `muructl detour traffic [DETOUR] preview|all|share=N|class=NAME` and `--traffic` on `muructl detour run`: a detour takes previews only by default, and the other choices are an overlay named `detour:APP` that the detour owns, listed by `muructl overlay list` with the source `detour` and ended with the detour. `all` and `share` wait for a rollout of the application to end.
