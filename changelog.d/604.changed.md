The daemon's process title is `muru daemon SCOPE` and the edge's `muru edge SCOPE`, with `muru-daemon` and `muru-edge` as their short names, SCOPE being `user` or `system`, or the daemon's state directory when it is not the default, as with `--state-dir`. In `muructl status --processes` the daemon's row says the same, as `user daemon, state ~/.local/state/muru`, and a host's row gives its release's part in routing after its name: `serving`, `canary 10%`, `held`, `draining` or `retained until 07:40`, which `--json`, `/v1/processes` and the dashboard's Processes section carry as the host's `state`.
