The node budget's `memory` and `cpu` default to the daemon's own cgroup's limits, `memory.max` less the headroom and the quota of `cpu.max`, read at each decision, when `[daemon.budget]` leaves them out, as in a container or under a systemd unit with `MemoryMax=` or `CPUQuota=`; `muructl daemon status` says which came from the cgroup. A worker of a group with a `hard_limit` counts at it in the budget.
